Cowon D2 Touch Areas Guide:

While Playing Screen:

# Central Part of Album Art
Menu: Short Press
Quickscreen: Long

# Track & Artist Text (Below Album Art)
Browse: Short
Context Menu: Long

# Shuffle Widget
Toggle Shuffle modes: Short

# Playing Status Widget
Play/Pause: Short
Stop: Long

# Left Area of Progress Bar
Previous: Short
REWIND: Long

# Right Area of Progress Bar
Next: Short
FFW: Long

# Repeat Widget
Toggle repeat Modes: Short

# Lower Volume Widget Area
Volume Down: Short and Long

# Upper Volume Widget Area
Volume Up: Short and Long
#
# Progressbar Area
Progress positioning
