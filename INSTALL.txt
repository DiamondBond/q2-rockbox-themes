Q2 Rockbox theme collection
===========================

1. Unzip this archive onto the card's root, next to .rockbox. The themes'
   files merge into the existing .rockbox.
2. On the player: Settings > Theme Settings > Browse Theme Files, then pick a
   theme and load it.

Requires the Shanling Q2 Rockbox port, and Q2 Pod V8.3 or later to boot it:

    https://github.com/DiamondBond/rockbox
    https://github.com/DiamondBond/q2-pod

Themes ported to 375x320 from themes.rockbox.org. Credits and licence notes
are still being completed.
