Q2 Rockbox Themes

Unofficial ports of Rockbox themes to the Shanling Q2 (375×320), remapped from their original layouts rather than squashed, and kept clear of the glass's rounded corners.

Install: put the Q2 Rockbox port and Q2 Pod V8.3+ on the player, unzip a theme onto the card's root next to .rockbox, then pick it in Settings › Theme Settings › Browse Theme Files. The full pack installs every theme at once.

Ports by DiamondBond/q2-rockbox-themes. Themes are by their original authors on themes.rockbox.org; each card links its source. Licences: CC-BY-SA 3.0 unless noted.
